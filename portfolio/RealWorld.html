Exposure Monitoring

01 · The Challenge

One book of risk, scattered across a dozen disconnected tools.

Underwriters and risk managers tracked marine, property, aviation, energy, cargo and ports exposures across siloed spreadsheets and single-purpose tools. There was no single view of accumulated exposure, and live events, sanctions, AIS silence, severe weather, region breaches, surfaced too slowly to act on.

02 · The Solution

One intelligence platform, eight purpose-built risk workspaces.

Real World consolidates exposure aggregation, sanctions and compliance screening, live alerting and deep analytics into one workspace, with geospatial context where location matters. Each line of business gets a purpose-built workspace that shares one design language, navigation model and component library, so a marine underwriter and an aviation risk manager feel immediately at home in each other's screens.

03 · Impact

Real-time exposure monitoring across eight portfolios at global scale, with sanctions, weather and breach alerts surfaced the moment they occur.

02.1 · The Product

A guided walk through Real World

Across eight specialised risk workspaces, every screen shares the same logic: portfolios on the left, aggregated exposure in the centre, live risk on the right, and the same drill-down path from portfolio to single asset.

01 / Unified Dashboard

The whole book at a glance

The landing dashboard greets each risk manager with headline KPIs for total exposure, active policies, live risk alerts and global regions, then routes them into Marine, Property, Aviation and Offshore/Energy through large, asset-counted entry cards.

02 / Marine

A live fleet under continuous watch

The marine workspace plots the entire insured fleet on a global chart. Selecting a vessel opens an at-a-glance voyage card covering route, speed, draught, flag and policy type, while regional aggregation and a sanctions / AIS-silence alert rail keep accumulation in check.

AIS TrackingVoyage DataExposure Aggregation
app.realworld.skytek.com / marine

03 / Vessel Detail

Everything known about a single hull

Drilling into a vessel unifies policy details, port calls, a 0–100 risk score with year-on-year trend, inspection history, environmental CII ratings and a scrubbable AIS track. It is the deepest view in the platform, organised so nothing competes for attention.

Risk ScoreEnvironmentalTracking
app.realworld.skytek.com / marine / vessel

04 / Cargo

Declarations screened against AIS

Cargo voyage analysis turns an uploaded declarations workbook into a screened portfolio, with every vessel checked against AIS for voyage progress and breach of a stated region. Completed, started, pending and breached voyages roll up into a summary, with each route inspectable on the map.

Voyage BreachWorkbook UploadClash Alerts

05 / Aviation

From global airspace to a single gate

Aviation reuses the same spatial grammar for a different asset class: portfolio exposure by airport and region, live NOTAMs and risk-area levels, then an airport drill-down with aircraft-on-the-ground, incoming traffic and summary statistics over a satellite view.

Airport ExposureNOTAMsDrill-Down
app.realworld.skytek.com / aviation

The airport view answers the underwriter's real question: how much value is sitting on the ground right now? It answers with portfolio aircraft, exposure by coverage type and a live map of arriving and parked aircraft.

/ aviation / LAX

One level deeper, the aircraft record carries hull and liability values, operator and lease details, utilisation and maintenance status, and the airports it has recently touched, so a single tail number can be assessed without leaving the workspace.

/ aviation / aircraft

06 / Property

Catastrophe exposure, geolocated

The property workspace maps insured value against high-value markets and live weather risk across wildfire zones, hurricane tracks and seismic zones, so concentration in a cat-exposed region is visible the instant a threshold is breached.

Cat RiskWeatherRegional Values
app.realworld.skytek.com / property

Opening a single property gives valuation, construction year and portfolio membership alongside a satellite location view, current conditions and a risk radar that flags live weather threats to that exact address.

07 / Nat Cat Events

Damage assessed from orbit

Nat Cat coverage spans every major peril the book is exposed to, from wildfires and windstorms to earthquakes, floods and tropical cyclones. When an event hits, the view sets the hazard perimeter over post-event satellite imagery, grades every property inside it from undamaged to completely destroyed, and lists the insured addresses caught within the footprint alongside an RDS classification and exposure figure.

Event FootprintDamage GradingBefore / After
/ nat-cat / event

Zooming to an individual address swaps the map for a draggable before-and-after comparison, so an adjuster can confirm the state of a single roof against pre-event imagery without leaving the claim.
