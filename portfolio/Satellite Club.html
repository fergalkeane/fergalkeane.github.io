01 / The Problem

Space data the insurance market could not actually use.

The problem was access. An underwriter pricing a Gulf tanker risk, or a claims manager assessing a hurricane loss, does not want a data feed, a tasking request form or a GIS tool. They want a defensible answer about a specific location, quickly, in language that survives being put in front of a client or a court.

02 / Framing

Design it as a club, not a data portal.

The reframe that shaped everything: this is a membership, not a subscription to imagery. Members get commissioned proprietary reports from the Skytek expert panel, image acquisition tools, advice on cost-effective tasking, priority access to major-event reports, workshops, and expert witness support.

That framing solved a hard positioning question. Sold as software, it competes with GIS platforms on features it does not want to compete on. Sold as a club with an expert panel behind it, the product is judgement, and the technology is how the judgement gets made.

03 / The Hard Constraint

Two audiences, one page, opposite needs.

A first-time visitor needs to understand what Earth Observation does for their book of business inside about fifteen seconds. An existing member arrives looking for the report published this morning on the Strait of Hormuz.

Rather than splitting into a marketing site and a separate logged-in app, the structure runs the proposition top-down and puts the live reports feed high on the same page. Login is a lightweight overlay instead of a destination, so a returning member never has to travel through the pitch to reach the intelligence.

04 / Outcome

Satellite imagery paired with expert analysis, delivered to the market that has to price the risk.

02.2 / Design Principles

Three rules for designing evidence

This content ends up in pricing decisions, claims files and legal submissions. That raises the bar on how it is presented.

01

Imagery is evidence, not decoration

Every satellite image carries its location, capture dates and provider. Nothing is cropped or colour-graded in a way that would not survive scrutiny.

02

Recency is a first-class signal

In a fast-moving war-risk or catastrophe scenario, a report from ten days ago is a different product to one from this morning. Dates are given the same weight as titles.

03

Insurance language, not space language

Aggregation, exposure, hull and cargo, renewal. The interface speaks the reader's vocabulary, and keeps the orbital mechanics behind the curtain.
