01 / The Challenge

A breakthrough device that needed to be trusted at a glance.

Diabetic foot ulcers are devastating and expensive. In the USA alone they drive roughly 75,000 amputations a year, with a treatment cost estimated near $17 billion. BlueDrop's home device scans a patient's feet daily and flags damage early, but as a young start-up they had no identity to carry that promise to clinicians, patients and investors.

The brand had to feel clinically credible and genuinely human at once: serious enough for the medical world, warm enough for someone managing a lifelong condition at home.

02 / The Solution

One symbol that holds the whole patient journey.

The identity is built on a single mark: a water drop, standing for “BlueDrop” and the clinical, diagnostic world, fused with a continuous loop standing for the lifelong patient journey, centred on an eye-like target for early detection. From that symbol flows a calm medical-blue palette, a clean confident wordmark, and a website that turns dense clinical evidence into something approachable.

03 / Impact

A complete identity system, from symbol to website, giving an early-detection start-up the credibility to win clinicians, patients and funding.

02.1 / The Symbol

A mark with meaning built in

The symbol is an equation: the company, an innovative start-up detecting diabetic foot ulcers, plus the patient journey, lifelong and often life-changing. BlueDrop sits at the heart of that journey, present at every step.

Concept

Company + Patient Journey = BlueDrop

Meaning

Drop · Loop · Detection

The drop grounds the name and the clinical world. The loop represents the continuous, lifelong patient journey. At the centre, a target / eye signals the product's core purpose: catching damage early, before it escalates.

02.3 / The Website

Clinical evidence, made approachable

The marketing site had to speak to three very different audiences (patients, clinicians and investors) on one page. The design leads with a hopeful, human message, then layers in the science, the technology and the team behind it.

01 / Homepage

A hopeful opening, grounded in evidence

“Taking giant leaps forward… one step at a time” sets an optimistic tone, then the page steps down into the hard clinical case for early detection and a clear look at the device itself.
