01 / The Context

A learning platform where the hardest problems are not educational.

atingi provides free courses and certificates to learners across emerging markets, designed with more than 370 partners and funded through German development cooperation. Its stated commitment is digital learning that is inclusive, accessible, relevant and safe.

The scale is real: over 550 courses, more than 30 course languages, 1.43 million registered learners and 647,000 certificates issued. That scale also means almost none of the usual assumptions about a learner's device, connection or reading language hold.

02 / The Constraints

Design for the worst connection in the room.

A learner may be on a low-cost Android phone, on mobile data they pay for by the megabyte, in a language read right to left, in a place where the connection drops mid-lesson. Every one of those is common rather than exceptional.

So the constraints were set as hard rules early: interfaces that hold up at small viewport widths, weight budgets that treat imagery as expensive, layouts that survive a language expanding by forty percent in translation, and states designed for interruption rather than assuming a clean session.

03 / The Approach

Three learners, one platform, different first questions.

Rather than one generic entry point into a catalogue of 550 courses, discovery was built around the question each cohort actually arrives with. A student asking what career suits them, an entrepreneur trying to turn an idea into a business, and a professional keeping pace with a changing job market need different first screens, not different products.

Certificates carry disproportionate weight. For many learners the credential is the reason to finish, so certification is treated as a visible destination from the first course page rather than a reward revealed at the end.

04 / Trade-offs

What we gave up to keep it usable.

Rich media was rationed. Video-heavy course design looks better in a pitch and performs worse for the actual learner, so text-and-image sequences with optional media became the default pattern rather than the fallback.

Partner flexibility was also deliberately limited. With hundreds of organisations contributing content, letting each one design its own course presentation would have fragmented the experience, so partners work inside fixed templates. Less freedom for contributors, a consistent and predictable experience for learners.

05 / Impact

1.43 million registered learners and 647,000 certificates issued, at no cost to the learner.

02.2 / Learner Cohorts

Discovery starts from the learner's own words

Each route is labelled with the statement a learner would make about themselves, not with an internal content category.

01

Students

“I need vocational orientation.”

Unsure which profession fits, or missing the skills a target role requires. The route leads to vocational training and in-demand skills rather than to a search box.

02

Entrepreneurs

“I want to start my own business.”

An idea exists but the business and financial skills to run it do not yet. Courses are sequenced in the order a founder actually needs them.

03

Professionals

“I want to unleash my full potential.”

Already working, keeping pace with a shifting job market. Designed for short, repeatable sessions that fit around a job rather than replace one.

02.3 / Design Principles

Four rules that come from the mission, not from taste

Inclusive, accessible, relevant and safe is the platform's own commitment. These are the working translations of it.

01

Inclusive by construction

Gender and inclusion are explicit programme goals, so imagery, examples and language are checked for who they exclude before they are judged on how they look.

02

Accessible on the cheapest device

Touch targets, contrast and type scale are set for a small low-cost Android screen in daylight. If it works there it works everywhere else.

03

Relevant to the local context

Courses are built with partners for specific markets. Interface copy avoids assumptions about schooling, employment structures or banking access.

04

Safe to use and to leave

Registration asks for the minimum, progress is never lost to a dropped connection, and nothing pressures a learner into sharing more than the course requires.

02.4 / The Platform

From first visit to issued certificate

The journey has four decisive moments: understanding what atingi is, finding a relevant course, registering, and completing enough to earn the certificate. Everything else is in service of those four.

01 / Home

Free, relevant and certified, stated immediately

The homepage answers what atingi is, what it offers and why it is credible, in that order, then routes visitors by cohort. Free with no hidden costs is stated plainly, because for this audience it is the deciding fact.

02 / Course Discovery

550 courses, narrowed without a wall of filters

Listing and detail pages share a fixed order: what you will learn, how long it takes, which language it is in, and what certificate it carries. Consistency across hundreds of partner-contributed courses does more for findability than any filter panel.
