Fergal Keane

Fergal Keane is an Irish artist known for his distinctive approach to visual art. Born in County Wexford, Keane's early life was deeply influenced by the landscape and historic narratives of his surroundings.

Keane was always drawn to creative expression, and his passion led him to pursue formal education in the arts and a career in design. His first introduction to art was under the tutorship of well-renowned artist Eamonn Carter, a founding member of the Gorey Arts Centre. The north Wexford area has become a melting pot of creativity mostly due to Carter and others.

Keane's art has been exhibited in galleries in Wexford and Dublin. He now lives and works in the picturesque village of Stepaside in the foothills of the Dublin mountains.
