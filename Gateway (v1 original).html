01 — The Challenge

Addressing complex usability hurdles in a high-stakes environment.

Users were switching between 12+ different software tools to complete a single maintenance cycle, leading to high error rates and significant cognitive fatigue in space mission support control centers.

02 — The Solution

Strategic design intervention focused on user outcomes.

Developed a unified “Workspace” concept within Gateway that dynamically serves only the relevant datasets and actions based on the user's specific progress in the maintenance lifecycle.

03 — Impact

Reduced operational friction by 35% and cut down the average time-to-completion for mission-critical tasks by 10 minutes per cycle.
